## The problem

Every AI session starts from scratch. You re-explain your project, your conventions, your constraints. The AI produces generic code. You repeat yourself. Sprint after sprint.

**Giving your agent a persona doesn't fix this.** "You are a Senior Developer" changes the tone — not the quality.

What actually works: **structured context that persists.**

---

## The solution: 5 pillars, one directory

`lytos init` creates a `.lytos/` directory in your project. Your AI reads it at the start of every session.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Intent" icon="document">
    Your project's constitution — the why, the stack, the constraints. The AI knows what it's building and why.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Design" icon="puzzle">
    9 reusable procedures — code review, testing, deployment, security. The AI follows steps, not vibes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Standards" icon="approve-check">
    Quality criteria — 300 lines max, no magic numbers, tests required. Verifiable, not subjective.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Progress" icon="rocket">
    Issues and sprint — a kanban board in markdown. The AI knows what to work on and what's blocked.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Memory" icon="star">
    Architecture decisions, patterns, bugs solved. The AI remembers what it learned — across sessions.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## 3 minutes to install

```bash
npx lytos-cli init
```

The CLI detects your stack, creates the `.lytos/` directory, and pre-fills the manifest. Then open your AI tool and say: **"Help me configure Lytos for this project."**

Everything is markdown. No API, no account, no telemetry. Your project context lives in git — not in a vendor's chat history.
